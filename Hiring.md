## 🔥 Top Interview Practice Platforms

These websites are great for preparing for technical interviews, whether it's through mock interviews, coding challenges, or practical exercises.

- **Tech Mock Interview** 💬
  A platform for practicing technical interviews with a simulated environment.
  `https://techmockinterview.com/`

- **Exercism** 🧑‍💻
  Offers a wide variety of coding tracks with mentorship and feedback.
  `http://exercism.org/tracks`

- **Interviewing.io** 🗣️
  Provides anonymous mock interviews with real engineers from top companies.
  `https://interviewing.io/`

- **LeetCode** 🧩
  A classic platform for practicing a vast number of DSA problems.
  `https://surl.lt/defltz`

- **HackerRank** 📝
  Features coding challenges and tutorials to prepare for interviews.
  `https://surl.li/ytwpgb`
