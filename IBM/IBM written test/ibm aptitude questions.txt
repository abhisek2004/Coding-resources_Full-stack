7)a printer begin printing at 7:54 am,at the rate of 104 forms per min. another printer begin at 8:20am. printing 117 forms per min. at what time will the two printer have printed the same number of forms

ANS:
--------
first printer starts at 7.54
2nd printer starts at 8.20
difference is 26 mins
during this 26 mins first printer prints (26*104) = 2704 forms
take the time as 'x' in which both printer prints same no of forms.
so...
117*x = 2704+(104*x)
13x = 2704
x = 208 mins
which is 3 hrs 28 mins...
so 8.20 + 2.28 = 11.44am
